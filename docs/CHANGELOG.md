# Release notes

Thanks to thejerf for [Baiji](../README.upstream.md) and to iChris4 for dolphinXR, which Baiji VR is built on.

## Baiji VR 0.9.1 (beta)

- **Report a Problem** in the Help menu and in the VR menu's settings. Nothing is sent unless you press **Send Report**
- After a crash, the next start offers a report
- The logs of the last 8 runs are kept
- A support link

## Baiji VR 0.9.0 (beta)

### Highlights

- Redesigned VR menu with a Material Design UI
- Start another GameCube or Wii game without leaving VR
- Stereoscopic 3D and Immersive 3D, with natural 3D matched to your IPD
- Live switching between VR and Flat
- Custom controls for every GameCube and Wii controller
- Mods, cheats and texture packs in the app
- Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance (USA) no longer freezes or turns white

### VR menu

- **Global** and **Per-Game** settings
- Quick saves, save states with pictures, and Autosave
- **Show Stats**: **In Front** or **On Wrist**
- Warnings and errors show in the headset

### Games

- Covers, banners, search, sorting and favourites
- **Download Thumbnails** from libretro-thumbnails, or use your own image
- **Add Folder**

### 3D

- Hold **L3** for 1 second to switch between Stereoscopic 3D and Immersive
- Sharper Immersive mode
- **Screen Curve**; move and resize the screen with the grips

### Controls

- A picture of each controller with its mapping
- **Edit Controls** for every game or for one game
- The right controller for well-known games
- Quick Save, Quick Load, Fast Forward and Stop, in VR and Flat

### Mods

- 37 cheats, patches and game settings, 3 mods and 24 texture packs, from their original source
- 16:9 in 12 games, and 60 FPS in Super Mario Sunshine and Pikmin 2
- Fixes for Eternal Darkness, F-Zero GX, Luigi's Mansion, Metroid Prime, Star Fox Adventures, The Last Story, Skyward Sword and Wii Sports Resort
- A game's built-in patches

### Qt menu

- Fixed Grid View, with covers
- `baiji-uninstall` can copy your saves to Dolphin

### Experimental, off by default

- **Dynamic Resolution**, **Pin Emulation Threads**, **Performance Hint**, **Non-Blocking EFB Copies**, **Non-Blocking Perf Queries** and **Skip Overwritten Clears**
